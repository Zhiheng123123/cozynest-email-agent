# CozyNest Home — Customer Service Policy

CozyNest Home is a small online store selling blankets, throws, and small rugs.
Customer service email: support@cozynest-home.example

## 1. Shipping
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Mon–Fri).
- Standard shipping (US): 3–7 business days. Free on orders over $50; otherwise $5.99.
- Express shipping (US): 1–3 business days, $14.99.
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
- If tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days, we open a carrier investigation and reply within 1 business day.
- We currently ship within the United States only.

## 2. Order Changes and Cancellations
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 12 hours of purchase, before they ship.
- After an order ships, it cannot be cancelled; the customer can return it instead (see Section 3).

## 3. Returns
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags.
- Not returnable: clearance/final-sale items and items marked "personalized".
- To start a return, the customer replies with their order number and reason; we email a return label.
- Return shipping: free if the item is defective or we sent the wrong item; otherwise $6.95 is deducted from the refund.

## 4. Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
- Original shipping fees are not refundable unless the item was defective or wrong.

## 5. Exchanges
- Exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days, subject to stock.
- If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference.

## 6. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items
- Customers should report damage or wrong items within 7 days of delivery, with a photo.
- We offer a free replacement or a full refund (customer's choice), including shipping costs.

## 7. Product Care
- Most blankets: machine wash cold, gentle cycle, tumble dry low. Do not bleach.
- Wool and faux-fur items: spot clean or dry clean only.
- Rugs: vacuum regularly; spot clean with mild soap and water.

## 8. Complaints and Escalation
- All complaints receive a personal reply within 1 business day.
- Goodwill options the team may offer (manager approval required): 10% discount code, free return shipping, or partial refund up to 20%.
- Agents must never promise refunds or compensation beyond this policy without human approval.

## 9. Contact Hours
- Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM ET. Emails received outside these hours are answered the next business day.
